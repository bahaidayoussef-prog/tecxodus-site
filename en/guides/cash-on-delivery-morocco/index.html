Cash on delivery in Morocco: refusals, returns and cash reconciliation

COD drives sales in Morocco, but every refused parcel costs picking, delivery and return. Here is how to keep refusals and cash gaps under control.

By Youssef B., founder of Tecxodus · Published 18 November 2026 · Version française

In short: most online orders in Morocco are paid in cash on delivery. Customers can refuse a parcel at no cost, so the seller pays for picking, delivery and often the return. The levers: confirm orders fast, ship fast, state the exact amount up front, restock returns quickly and reconcile cash order by order.

Order confirmation

Before an order is picked, the customer is called or messaged on WhatsApp to confirm the product, the total amount and the address. Confirmation should happen the same day, ideally within the hour. Use clear statuses (confirmed, call back, unreachable, postponed, cancelled) and a rule such as three attempts over two days before cancelling. At Tecxodus, only confirmed orders are released for picking.

What a refusal costs

With sample Tecxodus rates (MAD 0.50 per order line, MAD 30 per delivery), a refused one-item parcel costs at least MAD 30.50, plus any return fee. On 1,000 parcels with a 20% refusal rate, that is MAD 6,100 spent without a sale. The right metric is logistics cost per paid order, not per shipped order.

The return loop

  1. The courier records the failure reason: refused, unreachable, address not found, postponed.
  2. A new attempt is made or the parcel is sent back, according to the agreed rule.
  3. The parcel returns to the warehouse and is matched to its order.
  4. Inspection: packaging intact, item sellable, accessories complete.
  5. Back to sellable stock, or quarantined.
  6. Stock and order status updated for the seller.

Cash reconciliation

The courier collects the cash, hands it to the depot, the amounts are matched to delivered orders and the total is remitted to the seller, net of agreed fees. Insist on a statement per order: order and tracking numbers, delivery date, expected and collected amount, fees, final status. Watch for delivered-but-not-remitted parcels, amount differences and parcels “in return” that never come back.

Frequently asked questions

Why do Moroccan customers refuse COD parcels?

Impulse purchases, long lead times, an amount different from what was expected, unreachable customers or incomplete addresses, and products that differ from the picture.

How much does a refused parcel cost?

At least picking and delivery: MAD 30.50 for a one-item parcel at sample Tecxodus rates, plus any return fee.

How is COD cash remitted to the seller?

The courier collects, the depot reconciles amounts with delivered orders and remits the total net of fees, with a statement per order.

Does Tecxodus collect cash on delivery?

Yes. Cash is collected on delivery, reconciled order by order and remitted to the seller under the terms of the contract.
