What Tecxodus handles

  • Store integration: Shopify, YouCan, WooCommerce and Jumia connected to our WMS, TMS and control tower.
  • Inbound and storage: receiving and checking by SKU, put-away and live available stock. 100 to 20,000 m² in Aïn Sebaâ, Casablanca.
  • Pick and pack: trained pickers working to international standards, GS1 labelling, pre-shipment checks.
  • Delivery: own delivery team in Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier and the other major cities; partner carriers (Aramex, Amana, CTM, Ozon, Sendit, Cathedis) for the rest of the country.
  • Cash on delivery: collection, order-level reconciliation and remittance to the seller.
  • Returns and reverse logistics: inspection, restocking or quarantine, WMS status update.

Selling into Morocco as a foreign brand

International brands often reach Moroccan consumers through a local distributor, which means giving up margin and control of the customer. With a local fulfillment partner, the brand keeps its own store or marketplace account and sells directly:

  1. Import: your freight forwarder and customs broker bring the goods to Casablanca; Tecxodus coordinates the inbound appointment.
  2. Storage: goods are received, checked and stored by SKU, with live stock visible in the WMS.
  3. Orders: orders from your Shopify, WooCommerce or YouCan store, or from Jumia, flow into the WMS.
  4. Delivery and cash: parcels are delivered nationwide, COD is collected, reconciled and remitted.
  5. Reporting: stock, orders, deliveries, refusals and cash are tracked in one control tower.

Frequently asked questions

How much does e-commerce fulfillment cost in Morocco?

Sample Tecxodus rates: storage at MAD 3 per pallet per day, picking at MAD 0.50 per order line, delivery at MAD 30 anywhere in Morocco. The final quote depends on volumes, products and the services you select (packaging, returns, cash collection).

Which e-commerce platforms do you integrate with?

Shopify, YouCan, WooCommerce and the Jumia marketplace are integrated with the Tecxodus WMS, TMS and control tower. Orders flow into the WMS, available stock flows back to the store and every parcel is tracked to delivery.

Do you deliver across Morocco?

Yes. The Tecxodus delivery team covers Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier and the other major cities. The rest of the country is served through partner carriers: Aramex, Amana, CTM, Ozon, Sendit and Cathedis.

Do you handle cash on delivery (COD)?

Yes. Tecxodus runs the chain through to cash collection: the courier collects the order amount, collections are reconciled order by order and remitted to the seller under the terms of the contract.

Can a foreign brand sell in Morocco through Tecxodus?

Yes. Tecxodus receives your imported goods in coordination with your freight forwarder and customs broker, stores them in Casablanca, fulfils orders from your store or marketplace, delivers nationwide and collects COD payments.

What volume can you handle?

Volumes vary by client. Tecxodus operations are sized to process up to 1,000,000 orders per year, with pickers trained to international picking and labelling standards.

Which companies work with Tecxodus?

Jumia, Marjane, Mars, L’Oréal, LG, Samsung, Oraimo, Dell and the Moroccan Ministry of Health have entrusted logistics operations to Tecxodus.

How do returns work?

Refused or returned parcels come back to the warehouse, are inspected, put back into sellable stock or quarantined, and their status is updated in the WMS.
